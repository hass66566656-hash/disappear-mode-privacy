Privacy Policy

Disappear Mode — Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. Introduction

Disappear Mode ("the App") lets you write short notes that automatically disappear after a timer you choose. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what the App does and does not do with your information.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not require an account, login, phone number or email address. We do not collect your name, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or usage analytics.

3. Where Your Notes Are Stored

All notes and settings you create are stored only on your own device in a local database. They are never uploaded to any server, and we have no access to them. When a note's timer ends, it is permanently deleted from the local database. If you uninstall the App, all of its data is removed from your device.

4. Internet, Permissions and Third Parties

  • The App works completely offline and does not use the internet.
  • The App does not request any sensitive device permissions.
  • The App does not contain advertisements, analytics tools, trackers or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • We do not sell, share, rent or trade any information because we do not collect any.

5. Data Security

Because your data stays on your device, its protection depends on your device's own security (screen lock, device encryption and operating system updates). We recommend keeping your device locked and up to date. Please avoid storing highly sensitive information such as passwords or financial details in any notes app.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

7. Your Control

You can delete any note at any time, or remove all notes from the Settings screen. Notes also delete themselves automatically when their timer ends.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
